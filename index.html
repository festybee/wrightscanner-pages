Privacy Policy

WrightScanner

Effective date: November 23, 2026

WrightScanner ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by Pathwright Ltd, London, United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and your choices. It's written to match exactly what the app does today, not a generic template.

The short version

  • Your scans stay on your phone. Scanning, cropping, filters and text recognition all run on your device.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in with Google, it's so you can back up your scans to your own Google Drive. The backup goes straight from your phone to your Drive. Pathwright Ltd has no server, never receives your scans, and can't see them.
  • The backup sits in a private app folder in your Drive that only WrightScanner can open. The app can't see or touch anything else in your Drive.
  • You can delete your backup, or any scan in it, at any time, from inside the app or from Google Drive.
  • We don't show ads and don't use analytics or tracking tools. If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Information the app handles

Your scans and documents

Every page you scan or import, the text recognised on it, its name, the filter you chose and the folders you file it in are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Deleting a scan in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes it from the phone.

The app opts out of Android's own app-data backup, so your scans aren't copied to Google by Android itself. The only copy anywhere else is a WrightScanner backup, and only if you turn it on (see "Backup to your Google Drive" below).

Google sign-in (optional)

You can use the whole app without signing in. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with the app. They're kept on your phone, to show which account you're signed in with. Pathwright Ltd doesn't receive or store them. There is no WrightScanner account on any server of ours: your Google account is the account.

Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

When you first back up, the app asks for one extra Google permission: to "see, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive" (the Drive drive.appdata permission). This lets WrightScanner use a hidden app folder in your Drive. The folder isn't shown in your Drive file list, and the app can't open, list or change any of your other Drive files.

A backup copies to that folder:

  • the page images of your scans,
  • each scan's name, date, filter, recognised text and folder,
  • your folder names,
  • scans you deleted from a phone in the last 30 days ("Recently deleted"), so a mistaken delete can be undone.

These are sent directly from your phone to Google Drive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, using your Google sign-in. They're stored under your Google account, count towards your Google storage, and are protected by Google's security and Google's Privacy Policy. Pathwright Ltd never receives a copy and has no access to your Drive.

Backups run when you tap Back up now, or automatically if you choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly. Automatic backups run only on Wi-Fi. Restore downloads scans from the backup onto a phone signed in to the same Google account.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan, and only while you're scanning:

  • The standard scanner uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs on your device through Google Play services.
  • ID card mode and the optional "New camera (beta)" use the camera directly to show the live preview and take the photo. Finding the page or card edges happens on your phone.

Photos are never uploaded except as part of a backup you turned on. The app doesn't use the microphone.

Photos you import

"From Photos" uses Android's Photo Picker. The app only sees the images you pick, not the rest of your gallery.

Exporting and sharing

When you share, save or print a PDF or image, the app hands the file to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. What happens to it then is between you and the app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.

Information collected by Google's ML Kit

Text recognition and the document scanner use Google's ML Kit, which runs on your device. Your images and their text are not sent to Google for this. ML Kit may send Google basic technical information (such as device model, Android version and how the ML features performed) to keep the features working and improve them. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

How we use information

Your information is used only to provide the app's features: to show your scans, recognise their text, show which Google account you're signed in with, and back your scans up to and restore them from your own Drive. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone.

WrightScanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties

  • Google provides sign-in, Google Drive (where your backup is stored), and ML Kit (the document scanner and text recognition). Each is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. If ads are added in future, this policy will be updated first to say what they collect.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Scans on your phone stay until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Your backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. A scan you delete on your phone moves to "Recently deleted" in the backup at the next backup and is removed for good 30 days later.
  • To delete one scan from the backup: Account → Backup → Details, then delete it (or "Delete now" under Recently deleted).
  • To delete the whole backup: Account → Backup → Details → Delete backup. Or choose Remove access to my Google account and tick "Also delete my backup from Google Drive".
  • Without the app: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → WrightScanner → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove WrightScanner's access to your Google account: Account → Remove access, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Signing out removes your name, email and photo from the app on that phone.

Step-by-step instructions are also at wrightscanner.pathwrightltd.com/delete-data. Because we hold none of your data, we can't delete it for you, but we're happy to help. Email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

WrightScanner isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

Scans on your phone are protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's own security. Backups travel to Google Drive over encrypted connections and are stored under your Google account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed so your documents never pass through a server of ours.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without signing in, and without backup.
  • Back up by hand only, or turn automatic backup off at any time.
  • Delete any scan, or your whole backup, at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Pathwright Ltd holds none of your scans or account details, so you can do all of this yourself in the app and your Google account. Contact us below if you need help.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or how it's used, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
