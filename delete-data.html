Delete your data

WrightScanner

By Pathwright Ltd

The short version

  • WrightScanner has no server and no account of its own. Signing in uses your Google account, and backups are kept in your own Google Drive.
  • So there's nothing held by Pathwright Ltd to delete: you can delete everything yourself, in the app or in your Google account, using the steps below.

What there is to delete

  • Your backup (if you turned backup on): your scans' page images, names, recognised text and folders, plus "Recently deleted" scans, in a hidden WrightScanner folder in your Google Drive.
  • WrightScanner's access to your Google account (sign-in, and permission to use its own Drive folder).
  • Scans on your phone, in the app's private storage.

Delete your backup

In the app

  • Open Account (your photo at the top of Home), then Backup → Details → Delete backup. You stay signed in.
  • Or tap Remove access to my Google account and tick Also delete my backup from Google Drive. This deletes the backup and signs you out.
  • To delete just one scan from the backup, open Backup → Details and tap its delete button.

Without the app

  • On a computer, open Google Drive signed in to the same Google account.
  • Click the Settings gear, then Settings → Manage apps.
  • Find WrightScanner, click Options → Delete hidden app data, and confirm.

Deleting the backup is permanent. Scans on your phone aren't touched.

Remove WrightScanner's access

  • In the app: Account → Remove access to my Google account.
  • Or in your Google account: open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose WrightScanner, and remove its access.

Removing access doesn't delete your backup by itself. Delete the backup first (above) if you want it gone too.

Scans on your phone

Delete a scan in the app (tap the ⋮ menu next to it, then Delete), or uninstall WrightScanner to remove every scan on that phone.
